How Expense Automation Works

Submit employee claims from smartphone receipts in 4 simple steps.

1

Snap Receipt Photo

Take a photo of any thermal receipt, Grab/Uber slip, or hotel folio.

2

Multimodal OCR

Vision models extract merchant name, total paid amount, taxes, and transaction date.

3

Category Mapping

Automatically associates the receipt to your company's expense products (e.g. Travel, Meals).

4

Submit for Approval

Draft expense line generated with original photo attached in the chatter for instant manager approval.
