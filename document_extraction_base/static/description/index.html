📦 The All-in-One AI Extraction Suite

Core Engine

document_extraction_base

Foundation engine providing multimodal PDF-to-image rasterization (PyMuPDF), Cloud Gateway management, BYOK OpenAI-compatible routing, quota monitors, and the extensible ai.document.extract.mixin.

Dependencies: base, mail
Sales Orders

document_extraction_sale_order

Extracts customer Purchase Orders (PO) into Sales Quotations. Reconciles customer info, customer reference, payment terms, delivery addresses, and matches ordered items with internal sales catalog pricelists.

Dependencies: document_extraction_base, sale
Vendor Bills

document_extraction_vendor_bill

Parses supplier invoices directly into draft Vendor Bills (account.move). Reconciles supplier VAT/Tax IDs, detects invoice references, due dates, line items, and surfaces real-time calculation discrepancies.

Dependencies: document_extraction_base, account
HR Expenses

document_extraction_hr_expense

Extracts employee travel, dining, fuel, and lodging receipts directly into Odoo Expenses (hr.expense). Recognizes merchants, transaction dates, tax breakdown, and maps to predefined expense categories.

Dependencies: document_extraction_base, hr_expense

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Does this module modify standard Odoo financial logic or invoice posting?

No. The module operates strictly at the draft ingestion layer. All ledger entries, taxes, journals, and sequence numbers follow 100% standard Odoo accounting workflows without altering core accounting rules.

Q: Is our financial and customer document data stored on third-party servers?

Never. When using Cloud Gateway, payloads are processed in-flight with zero data retention. When using BYOK mode, your Odoo instance communicates directly with your own AI endpoint (such as your private OpenAI key or self-hosted Ollama server) with zero intermediaries.

Q: Can developers extend the AI extraction engine to custom models?

Yes! By inheriting the abstract mixin ai.document.extract.mixin, developers can add automated multimodal extraction to any Odoo model (such as custom Bill of Lading, Customs CEISA forms, or Delivery Slips) with just a few lines of Python.
